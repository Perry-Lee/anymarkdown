AnyMarkdown 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 16 日

AnyMarkdown 是一款本地优先的 iOS 应用。我们的原则很简单：你的文档属于你，我们不收集、不上传、也看不到它们。

我们不收集任何数据

AnyMarkdown 没有服务器，不设账号系统，不接入任何统计分析、广告或第三方 SDK。应用不收集、存储或传输任何个人信息或使用数据。

你的文档保存在哪里

  • 所有导入的文件在设备本地完成转换（包括 PDF、Office 文档、图片文字识别等），转换结果以 Markdown 文件形式保存在应用的沙盒目录中。
  • 文字识别（OCR）使用 Apple 的 Vision 框架，完全在设备上运行。
  • 通过系统分享进入应用的内容，仅经由本机的 App Group 容器传递，不经过网络。

唯一联网的功能：链接导入

当你使用「从链接导入」时，应用会直接访问你提供的网址以抓取网页内容——就像浏览器打开该网页一样。请求只发往你输入的网站，不经过我们或任何第三方的服务器；抓取到的内容同样只保存在你的设备上。

内购与订阅

Pro 功能的购买、订阅与恢复均由 Apple 的 App Store（StoreKit）处理。我们无法接触你的付款信息；Apple 如何处理这些数据请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。

儿童隐私

应用不收集任何数据，因此也不会收集儿童的任何信息。

政策变更

若本政策发生变更，我们会在此页面更新并调整生效日期。重大变更会在应用更新说明中注明。

AnyMarkdown Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 16, 2026

AnyMarkdown is a local-first iOS app. Our principle is simple: your documents belong to you — we don't collect them, upload them, or ever see them.

We collect no data

AnyMarkdown has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or usage data.

Where your documents live

  • All imported files are converted on your device (including PDFs, Office documents, and image text recognition). The resulting Markdown files are stored in the app's private sandbox.
  • Text recognition (OCR) uses Apple's Vision framework and runs entirely on-device.
  • Content shared into the app via the system share sheet passes only through the local App Group container, never over the network.

The only online feature: Import from Link

When you use "Import from Link", the app fetches the URL you provide directly — just like a browser opening that page. The request goes only to the website you entered, never through our servers (we have none) or any third party. The fetched content is stored only on your device.

Purchases & subscriptions

Pro purchases, subscriptions, and restores are handled entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We never have access to your payment information. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles that data.

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no data at all, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
